# Mechanical simplifications

Where the model departs from a real gearbox, on purpose. Recorded for the "How it works" section
to draw on; nothing here is a bug to fix.

- The pressure plate's lift is exaggerated: about 9 mm drawn against about 2 mm in a real clutch.
- The dogs are shallower than real ones, with 4 mm of slider travel.
- The shafts are plain: no splines, no bearings (other than the clutch basket's bush), no circlips,
  bushes or thrust washers.
- No detent spring, no shift-shaft return spring and no fork compliance are drawn.
- The gear teeth are standard involutes with no profile shift; the 13/37 pair would interfere by a
  few micrometres.
- The chain advances one link per sprocket tooth.
- The rear axle is drawn level with the output shaft.
- The crankshaft is a stub.
- The primary driven gear has no damper springs.
- In the gear train, the lower fork positions and the collar widths are chosen for clarity.
